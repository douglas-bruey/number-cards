Assemble

Stack cards from the palette onto the build area to match the target pattern. Dots keep their colours — and may not overlap.

  • Drag a palette card (1–8) onto the build area.
  • While dragging, press Space to rotate 90° clockwise.
  • Overlapping dots bounce the card back to the palette.
  • Match the target to clear it. Clear the whole deck to win.
